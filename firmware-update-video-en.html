Firmware Update Video Guide

A simple step-by-step guide for updating your digital wall clock from a phone or computer.

!
Keep power connected. Do not unplug the clock or close the browser while an update is in progress. Use only firmware and filesystem files supplied for your clock model.

Tip: If the phone says “No internet connection” while connected to the clock WiFi, stay connected. The clock uses a local address and does not need internet for this update.
